# Worked example: BTC demo session, 2026-11-22

Account: Exness demo 434521570 (`BTCUSDm`, 1 lot = 1 BTC, spread $6.40). Balance $680.00 at the start,
$743.94 at the end (+$63.94, +9.4%). Four trades, four wins. Times are broker server time.

Read this as an example of the method, not as proof that it works. BTC fell about $700 in one direction
during the hour, and every trade was a sell. A sideways or reversing hour would have produced losses.

## The picture before trading

| Timeframe | What it showed |
|---|---|
| D1 | Ten days down, from 73,900 to 60,000. Price far under ema20 (67,700), RSI 23. |
| D1, previous day | Fell to 55,060, closed back at 60,084. A strong bounce, so a reversal was possible. |
| H4 | Under ema20 (60,660) and ema50 (63,580): still a downtrend. |
| H1 | The bounce stalled at 60,900, then went flat between 59,840 and 60,460. |
| M1 | ATR about $36. Lower highs: 60,123, then 60,087, then 60,054, then 60,036. |

Conclusion: the larger trend was down and the bounce had stalled under 60,900, so sells only, on pullbacks.

## The trades

| # | Type | Entry | Stop (distance) | Target (distance) | Lots | Risk | Exit | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Swing | Sell at market | 60,078 | 61,050 ($972) | 58,100 ($1,978) | 0.03 | $29 (4.3%) | 59,481, closed by the user on mobile | +$17.92 |
| 1 | Sell at market | 60,022 | 60,102 ($80) | 59,932 ($90) | 0.15 | $12 (1.8%) | Target, after 13 min | +$13.50 |

### Why each one

- **Swing.** Sell with the ten-day trend. The stop went just above the previous day's high (60,900), the one
  level that would have shown the bounce was continuing. The target was 2 times the stop, at the 57,700 to
  58,100 area where price had paused the day before.
- **Scalp 1.** Price had bounced from 59,876 to 60,087 and was falling back under the M1 ema50. Sold at
  market. Stop above the 60,087 high; target just above the 59,876 low. Price moved $62 in its favour, came
  all the way back to the entry, then reached the target. A stalled scalp is normal; the stop decides, not patience.
- **Scalp 2.** A big bar dropped price from 60,036 to 59,906, right onto the hour's low. Selling there would
  have meant a far stop and a near target, so the order was set to trigger only on a bounce to 59,972, the
  old support at 59,954 to 59,990. Stop above the last lower highs (60,036 to 60,054); target at the low.
- **Scalp 3.** Price broke the low and ran to 59,674 with no bounce. The first trigger at 59,905 was never
  reached and was dropped. After a new look, the trigger went to 59,762, just under the last small M1 high
  (59,769). Stop above the 59,840 bar high; target just under the 59,674 low.

### Orders that did not trigger

| Level | What happened |
|---|---|
| Sell at 59,905 | Price fell straight to 59,694 without bouncing. |
| Sell at 59,700 | Price fell to 59,450 without bouncing. |
| Sell at 59,600 | Price spiked to 59,273, snapped back to 59,587, and missed the level by $13. |
| Sell at 59,660 (smaller size, wider stop) | Never reached; session ended. |

The session stopped there. M1 ATR had gone from $36 to $100, and the 59,273 spike left a long lower wick on
heavy volume, which is how a fast move often ends.

## The numbers behind the rules

| Trade | M1 ATR | Stop ÷ ATR | Target ÷ stop | Spread ÷ stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 36 | 2.2 | 1.13 | 8% |
| 2 | 40 | 1.9 | 1.27 | 9% |
| 3 | 42 | 2.0 | 1.23 | 8% |
| Untriggered 4th | 64, then 100 | 1.7, then 1.3 | 1.18, then 1.38 | 6%, then 5% |

- Risk stayed near 2% of the account: when the stop widened, the lot size came down.
- Every stop sat beyond a real swing on the chart, never at a round distance.
- Every entry after the first waited for a pullback. Three of seven planned entries were filled.

## What the backtests say

The same day, nine simple 1-minute rules were tested on 1,000,000 BTC bars (Dec 2024 to Nov 2026): breaks of
20, 60 and 240-minute ranges, a trend-filtered break, the EMA 20/50/200 pullback, Bollinger and RSI fades,
and following or fading big bars. Before the spread they all averaged between -0.02R and +0.02R per trade.
With a $6.40 spread they lost 0.08R to 0.12R per trade, in both halves of the data. Three or four live wins
do not outweigh that.
